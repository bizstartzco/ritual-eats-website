Nothing in this kitchen is older than a day.

No freezer, no microwave, no pre-formed patties. Every burger starts as chuck and brisket at seven in the morning and is gone by closing.

The steel

230°C. Eighty-two seconds. Do not touch it.

The patty hits the steel as a loose ball and gets pressed once. The crust forms in under a minute and the spatula does the rest.

Buns from our own oven, brushed with beef fat and toasted on the flat-top.

From grind to griddle

Six moves. Twelve minutes. One burger.

01/ 06

Scroll through what happens between your order and your tray. The counter follows you.

Move 01

Grind

Chuck and brisket, 80/20, ground coarse at seven each morning. What is left at close goes to the staff meal, never to tomorrow.

80/20 blendGround 07:00
Move 02

Ball

Seventy-gram balls, rolled loose. Pack them tight and the patty turns dense. Loose meat makes lace.

70 gNever pressed early
Move 03

Smash

One press, all the way down, held for ten seconds. Parchment between steel and spatula so the crust stays on the meat.

230°C steel10 s press
Move 04

Scrape and flip

The spatula goes under at a low angle so every bit of crust comes with it. Salt and pepper only on the top side.

82 s per side
Move 05

Melt

American cheese, one slice per patty, covered for twenty seconds. A splash of water under the cloche for steam.

20 s under cloche
Move 06

Stack

Toasted bun, ritual sauce, pickles, white onion, patties, top bun. Wrapped in paper for two minutes so the bun steams soft.

Paper-wrapped2 min rest
